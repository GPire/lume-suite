# Video kit: three short videos that can carry the project

Video is what made CleanYourTV spread, and a TV is a thing you can film. Each video is 20 to 30 seconds, filmed with a phone pointing at the TV, no voice-over needed: on-screen text only, so one cut works in every language. No hashtags and no emoji in titles or captions.

## How to film

- Phone horizontal for YouTube, vertical for Shorts/Reels/TikTok (shoot the TV in the middle third; keep the sides clean).
- Turn the TV brightness down a little and room lights off: less glare, no moire. Lock the phone's exposure on the screen.
- Film the same stick before and after, same angle, same lighting. Real, not staged: if something stutters, show it.
- Record a second pass with the screen picture from Lume's own screens tool (`lume/tools/screens.sh`) for clean stills.
- Music: royalty-free or none. Never a famous track.

# The three 15-second videos: titles, captions, first comment

Made for Reels, Shorts and TikTok. Each one is a small story: a hook that stops the thumb, the pain made specific, a turn, a reward you can see and hear, and the address. No hashtags, no emoji. Files are release assets: `lume-short-1-home.mp4`, `lume-short-2-gallery.mp4`, `lume-short-3-airplay.mp4`.

## 1. Home screen (lume-short-1-home.mp4)

- **On screen:** *Your TV home screen is an ad.* then the real Fire TV home scrolling and stuttering (87% of frames dropped), *Same stick. Now watch.*, then Lume: *Calm. Smooth. Yours.* and *8% of frames dropped, from 87%*.
- **Title:** Your Fire TV home screen is an ad. Here is the same stick, fixed.
- **Caption:** Same Fire TV Stick, same buttons. The stock home dropped 87% of frames; Lume drops 8%. Free and open source: gpire.github.io/lume-suite
- **Pinned comment:** Which TV do you have? Tell us the model and what happened: github.com/GPire/lume-suite/discussions
- **Why it works:** an accusation the viewer already half believes (curiosity and a little anger), a number (specificity), a visible contrast on the same device (proof), three one-word beats (reward), a free promise at the end (peak-end).

## 2. Gallery (lume-short-2-gallery.mp4)

- **On screen:** *Your TV is a black rectangle 20 hours a day.* a dark screen and a counter, *Make it a gallery.*, then a Monet in its frame, a Homer, a Hokusai: *36 masterpieces.* *Bright by day. Warm at dusk. Dark at night.*
- **Title:** Turn your TV into a painting when it is off
- **Caption:** 36 public-domain masterpieces, shown in their frames, bright by day and dark at night. Free for Fire TV and Android TV: gpire.github.io/lume-suite
- **Pinned comment:** What painting should be on it first? We read every answer.
- **Why it works:** loss framing for something people never question (dead glass), a metaphor you can picture, beauty as the reward, a question in the comment to invite replies.

## 3. AirPlay (lume-short-3-airplay.mp4)

- **On screen:** *You play a song. The TV shows nothing.* a phone playing, a black TV, *Tap AirPlay. Pick the TV.*, then the song on the TV with the words in time: *Words, in time. Cover. Colour. Calm.*
- **Title:** Apple Music on your Fire TV, with the words
- **Caption:** Lume Cast is a free AirPlay receiver for your Fire TV. Pick the TV from your iPhone and the song shows up with its words. Free and open source: gpire.github.io/lume-suite
- **Pinned comment:** Which song would you put on first? Tell us your iPhone model too: it helps us test.
- **Honest note:** the song on screen is a sample made for the demo; the receiver was tested on a Fire TV Stick 4K Max with real songs; the phone picture is an illustration, not Apple's screen.

## How to post them

Day 1 the home screen, day 2 the gallery, day 3 AirPlay, then the long video. Post at the time your audience is on the sofa (evening, local time). Reply to every comment in the first hour. If a video is picked up, post the next one the same day.
